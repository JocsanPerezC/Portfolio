# Portfolio home (single page)

Scope: the whole one-page portfolio rendered by src/App.jsx. Mode: Experience.
Audience: recruiters deciding whether to interview. Action: open a project, download the CV, write a message.
Proof: three live projects with screenshots, live GitHub data, the interactive technologies sphere.
Constraints: bilingual ES/EN, light and dark themes, GSAP for motion, reduced motion respected. Must not feel overloaded or unprofessional. The user wants the name large with the role on first view.
Memorable moment: the spread opening with the name as a species heading facing a plate of numbered project specimens.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a Costa Rican field guide to one developer: plates of numbered specimens facing their legends. It refuses the dark hero, gradient name and row of glass cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white plate pages (#f2f5f1) bound in deep green bookcloth (#1e4d36) with a near-black spine (#14231a). Flat fields only, never gradients. Toucan orange (#f08a24) is reserved for index tabs and the active mark; morpho blue (#1d5fd1) is the one interactive accent. Square-cornered plates with 2px ink frames, black number chips on each specimen, a hairline classification table. Bricolage Grotesque display, Atkinson Hyperlegible text. Dark theme is the same book at night: bookcloth page, light ink.

STORY: The visitor learns who Jocsan is and what he does, sees three working products as specimens with their stack as classification, checks live GitHub field data, and writes or downloads the CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: An open spread on the green cloth with a central spine. Left page: kicker "Plate 1 · full-stack developer", the name at about 130px over two lines, a classification table (habitat, trade, status), and two buttons at the bottom. Right page: plate titled observed specimens, a large specimen 1 and two stacked ones, legend line below, orange tab index on the edge.

FORM: Field guide plate spread, candidate 7 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:c9cf9fff6e85d64e. Raises: flat colour only (ebru); state shown as a mark, not a hue (cutting bench); one governing axis, the spine (labanotation). Signature interaction: scrolling turns the guide's plates; the spine stays fixed while each section enters as the next plate, with GSAP scrub. Motion grammar: pages settle, chips stamp in, nothing floats.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
