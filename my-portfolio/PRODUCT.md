# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring teams at companies looking for a developer for a job or professional internship. They arrive from a CV, LinkedIn or a job application, scan quickly, and need to decide whether Jocsan is worth an interview: what he builds, with which technologies, and how to reach him.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Jocsan Perez Coto, a full-stack software developer from Cartago, Costa Rica, and student at the Tecnológico de Costa Rica. It exists to turn a recruiter's quick visit into a contact or an interview. Success means the visitor understands his skills and sees working projects within the first minute, then downloads the CV or writes to him.

## Positioning

A versatile full-stack developer who ships complete, deployed products end to end, from interface to database, rather than tutorials or mockups. Accessibility shows up in the work (PIELS, Web 3D Inclusiva) as evidence, but it is not the headline message.

## Operating Context

- Visitors come from a CV link, LinkedIn or GitHub, on desktop and on phones.
- Bilingual: Spanish and English, switchable on the page. English is the default.
- Light and dark themes, switchable on the page.
- Hosted on Vercel from this repository.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack: React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4, GSAP (required by the user for motion), Three.js via React Three Fiber, EmailJS.
- Sections that must stay: hero/about, featured projects, the interactive 3D technologies sphere, live GitHub stats (profile, repos, stars, highlighted repositories), contact form via EmailJS with email and CV download, social links.
- Removed by the user: the Kirby GIF animations are not part of the page.
- Projects shown: exactly three, in this order: PIELS, EDH Tournaments, Web 3D Inclusiva.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Product Principles

1. The work is the proof: live, clickable projects come before claims.
2. A recruiter should reach projects, CV and contact in one move from anywhere.
3. Show breadth of stack honestly, without inflated numbers.
4. Every section works in both languages and both themes.
